## Case decision — AUTH-021 (suite 031, Manual)

**BL-AUTH-016 wins.** Its human sources are:
- the VCST-6132 description plus the QA comment of 2026-10-14;
- the VCST-5496 resolution (Done);
- now the Customer 3.1030.0 release.

All three say a credential sign-in naming a blocked organization falls back to an accessible one. When none
remains and the org is **locked**, the sign-in succeeds with no organization. The `password` grant fell
back even before VCST-6132. AUTH-021 expects "Login denied … POST /connect/token returns 4xx", which is
stale. Change for test-management-specialist (`/qa-review-tests --fix`):
- **Business_Rule:** `BL-B2B-002; BL-AUTH-003` → `BL-AUTH-016; BL-AUTH-012`. BL-B2B-002 is organization
  pricing and BL-AUTH-003 is global lockout; both are mis-citations.
- **Preconditions:** a single-org user whose only membership is **locked** by the org admin; the storefront
  sends that org on sign-in.
- **Assertions:**
  - `[NETWORK] POST /connect/token` → 200, the token has no `organization_id` claim.
  - `[STATE]` signed in with no organization context: no org switcher, no Corporate navigation.
  - Not the global-lockout message.
  - A multi-org variant lands on an accessible org.
- **Failure_Signals:** the sign-in is refused, or the global lockout copy is shown.
- **Mind-map stamp:** `Behavior:auth.org.resolve.no-org-left` (the node "With every organization locked,
  the sign-in succeeds with no organization").
- The refusal itself belongs on an explicit `refresh_token` switch (BL-AUTH-012 check), not on this case.
