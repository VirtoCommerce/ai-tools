## Findings
EXP-1 Medium — account enumeration on anonymous /api/otp/request, otp_email grant and the storefront OTP form ("User not found. Ensure you've entered the correct information" vs success); password path stays generic. Evidence: screenshots/exp-storefront-otp-unknown-email.png, probe output above.
EXP-2 Low — a refused customer is challenged and emailed a code before the store rule refuses (after the code). (recorded per model #10)
EXP-3 Low — password step ignores CodeResendInterval, each attempt emails and kills the previous code.
EXP-4 Low — sign-in log drill-down hides its user filter (screenshots/exp-signin-log-awaiting.png).
Environment note (not a product finding): SendGrid "Maximum credits exceeded" — no MFA/OTP email is delivered on vcst.
