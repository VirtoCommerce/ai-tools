VCST-6056: UCP create_cart validates the combined quantity

env vcst-qa UCP 3.1007.0-pr-9-91ca PR vc-module-ucp#9 @ 91ca741 (open) XCart 3.1037.0-pr-141-fb27 Inventory 3.1008.0 Platform 3.1073.0-pr-3121-9965 verified 2026-09-29

Before the fix, create_cart checked each line_items[] entry against stock on its own and merged entries for the same product afterwards. Splitting 9 units into 4 + 5 on a product with 5 in stock gave a successful cart holding 9. The fix consolidates duplicate product ids (case-insensitively) before the first XCart mutation and sends the total, so XCart's stock rule sees 9 and the VCST-6054 refusal applies.

Before and after: create_cart, entries 4 + 5, stock 5
