## Risk Areas
- Nested plated widget (K2/S12/T-18/T-40): no Storybook story nests a widget that has its own plate (only `seamless` children), and the app has 0 (dashboard checked). The fork-override reach (G1) cannot be observed without writing to the page.
- Widget does not clip (`overflow: visible`), so corner-following depends on the consumer (`rounded-*-[inherit]` in the slot stories). Content painted to the plate edge in the app will cross the soft corners (the PR's own follow-up list).

## Observations
- Classic md widget with prepend-icon (coffee) is identical on QA and DEV: radius 8, border 1px, shadow, Lato 700 uppercase 18px, polygon 36px, insets 24. DEV writes no data-preset; QA writes it.
- Catalog filter skeleton → facets: 6 collapsed 40px skeletons (gap 20) become 16 facets (40px, gap 20). The column moves 18px because the category tree grows 1336→1354, not because of the skeleton.
- News: 9 md skeletons 397×222 (radius 8) become 4 cards 397×507 (rows grow, known VCST-6633). Article at 375: the lg skeleton sits inside the container padding (24..351) and drops 34px when the breadcrumbs mount.
- /account/coupons: its own `coupon-item-skeleton` shows for ~200 ms with no throttling. The account sidebar widgets change height 3× during load (293→285→357).
