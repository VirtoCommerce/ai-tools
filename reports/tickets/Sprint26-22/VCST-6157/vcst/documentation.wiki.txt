h2. Documentation — VCST-6157: Filter Sales Rep Hub tasks by customer and priority

h3. What's new
On the *Tasks* page of the Sales Rep Hub you can now narrow your task list by *customer* and by
*priority*. The *Customers* filter lists only the organizations you serve, and you see only your own
tasks for them. Filters combine: a task must match every filter you set. The list has new *Priority*
and *Customer* columns, and you can link any task to a customer in the *New task* and *Edit task*
dialogs.
Shipped in: VirtoCommerce.SalesRep@3.1018.0-pr-33-ff13 · VirtoCommerce.TaskManagement@3.1007.0-pr-33-14ff · platform@3.1094.0 · theme@2.62.0-pr-2637-02f2-02f26436

h3. How to set it up
* *Modules:* deploy Sales Rep and Task Management together, at the versions in _Shipped in_. The run verified the filters on that pair.
* *Sales Rep in the store:* in the back office, click *Stores*, select the store, open the *Settings* widget and turn *Sales rep enabled* on.
* *Customers of the rep:* in *Sales Reps*, open the rep and select the organizations they serve. Those organizations are what the *Customers* filter and the *Customer* field offer.

h3. How it works

h4. Find a customer's tasks
# In the account menu, open *Sales Rep Hub*, then *Tasks*. The page opens on today's tasks, with a *Due today* chip.
!doc-01-tasks-page.png|width=700!
# Click *×* on the *Due today* chip to see tasks for every date. Then click *All customers*. The list shows only the customers you serve.
!doc-02-customers-open.png|width=700!
# Check a customer. A chip with its name appears. The list and the *Due dates* calendar show only that customer's tasks.
!doc-03-customer-picked.png|width=700!

Check several customers to see the tasks of any of them.

!VCST-6157-filter-by-customer.gif|width=700!

h4. Narrow by priority
# Click *All priorities*. It offers *Lowest*, *Low*, *Normal*, *High* and *Highest*.
!doc-04-priorities-open.png|width=700!
# Check *Highest*. The list keeps only the tasks that match the customer *and* the priority.
!doc-05-priority-picked.png|width=700!
# To drop one filter, click *×* on its chip.

!VCST-6157-filter-by-priority.gif|width=700!

{panel:title=Tip|bgColor=#E3FCEF}
Priority names match exactly: *High* never shows *Highest* tasks, and *Low* never shows
*Lowest* ones.
{panel}

h4. Link a task to a customer
# Click *New task*. The *Customer* field starts empty, with *No customer*.
!doc-06-new-task.png|width=700!
# Enter a *Title*, then open *Customer*. It lists the customers you serve.
!doc-07-customer-list.png|width=700!
# Pick one customer, fill in the other fields, and click *Save*.
!doc-08-customer-set.png|width=700!
# The task shows the customer in the *Customer* column, and that customer's filter finds it at once.
!doc-09-task-with-customer.png|width=700!

!VCST-6157-task-customer.gif|width=700!

{panel:title=Note|bgColor=#DEEBFF}
*Edit task* has the same *Customer* field. Changing other fields keeps the customer; a task with
no customer shows *—* in the *Customer* column. *Schedule call* on a customer's page opens
with that customer already picked.
{panel}
