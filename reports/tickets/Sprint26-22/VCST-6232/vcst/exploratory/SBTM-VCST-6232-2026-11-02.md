## Risk Areas
- Revenue surfaces (cart coupons, card fields) hold most of the literal radii. Any design with a radius far from classic's 8px amplifies them.
- CMS content (Builder.io) is outside the design's reach by layer order, so a "design" is never storefront-wide on Builder pages.
- The Chromium-based diff misses `line-height: normal` shifts. The parity harness should also run once on Firefox.

## Model amendments (with evidence)
- **K7 split:** K7a CMS real headings (`h1–h6` in `.builder-block`) follow the heading role (Geologica). K7b CMS text blocks (`builder-text`, SDK unlayered style) keep the authored stack. Matrix L3/L4/L5 for K7b = "beaten by unlayered third-party CSS (README layer order)", L8 = classic unaffected only because the authored font equals classic's. Evidence: mission 1a.
- **K10:** L5 not observable on localhost (CSP `frame-ancestors`). Wrapper radius is a K6 literal on a revenue surface, so add it to row 4's evidence.
- **New carrier K13:** `line-height: normal` boxes (form inputs). The line box follows the family's metrics, so a family change moves it without any token (Firefox 19→20px).
- **Row 12:** resolved statically. Classic geometry is preserved; a design's shadow tokens are ignored by coloured shadows (B-3).
- **Row 18:** Cyrillic {OBSERVED} = design faces, no fallback. Greek not reachable (no Greek strings on the store).
- **K12:** still GAP live. Static reading confirms the unlayered `_custom.scss` and the replace semantics of `custom-fonts.ts`, including `[]`.
