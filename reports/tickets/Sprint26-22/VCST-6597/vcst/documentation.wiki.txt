h2. Documentation — VCST-6597: Each store decides who signs in with an emailed code

h3. What's new
Each store now chooses who must enter a one-time code from email after the password. Two new store modes
join *Use global setting*, *Enabled* and *Disabled*:
* *AdminManaged*: an administrator decides per customer and per company.
* *CustomerChoice*: customers turn two-factor authentication on or off for themselves, and a company maintainer can require it for every member.

Every storefront change is confirmed with a code sent by email. A customer whose own store requires the
code is asked for it even when the sign-in names no store or another store.
Shipped in: VirtoCommerce.MFA@3.1002.0-pr-3-1a0f · theme@2.62.0-pr-2622-caac-caac275a · platform@3.1094.0

h3. How to set it up
# In the back office, click *Stores*, select the store, and open the *SETTINGS* widget.
# Under *Store > Security*, pick a mode in *Multi-factor authentication*, click *OK*, then *Save*.
!doc-01-store-setting.png|width=700!

||Mode||Who needs the code||
|Use global setting|Whatever the global *Multi-factor authentication* setting says|
|Enabled|Everyone who signs in to the store|
|Disabled|Nobody among the store's own customers; Backoffice accounts and other stores' users follow the global setting|
|AdminManaged|Only customers and companies the administrator set to *Required*|
|CustomerChoice|Customers who turned it on, members of a company that requires it, and anyone set to *Required*|

* *Back-office permissions:* {{mfa:read}} shows the *Multi-factor authentication* widget on user and organization blades, read-only. {{mfa:manage}} also lets you change and save the mode.
* *Company switch:* maintainers need the {{xapi:my_organization:mfa:manage}} permission on their organization role. No stock organization role carries it.
* *Turning it on in the storefront* needs a confirmed email.

{panel:title=Confirm emails first|bgColor=#FFFAE6}
A user whose email is not confirmed cannot receive the code, so once MFA applies to them they cannot
sign in. Confirm the emails of the users before you require MFA for them or their company.
{panel}

h3. How it works

h4. An administrator sets a customer or a company
# In the back office, open the customer's account (or the organization) and click the *MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION* widget.
# Pick *Off*, *Enabled* or *Required* in *Mode*. *Effective status* shows, per store, whether the code will be asked and why; it updates as a preview before you save.
# Click *Save*. A *Required* mode cannot be turned off in the storefront.
!doc-02-admin-mode-blade.png|width=700!

In the Admin Shell the same page opens from the *MFA:* button of the user or organization blade.

h4. A customer turns two-factor authentication on (CustomerChoice store)
# Open the account menu, then *Security*. *Two-factor authentication* shows *Off*.
!doc-03-security-off.png|width=700!
# Click *Turn on*. A code is sent to your email; enter it and click *Confirm*.
!doc-04-turnon-code-step.png|width=700!
# The status shows *On*. *Turn off* works the same way, with a new code.
!doc-05-security-on.png|width=700!
# From the next sign-in, after your password the storefront asks for the code sent to your email.
!doc-06-signin-code.png|width=700!

!doc-gif-customer-turn-on.gif|width=700!

h4. A company maintainer requires it for every member (CustomerChoice store)
# Open *Company info*. Under *Two-factor authentication for the company*, click *Turn on* and confirm with the code sent to your email.
!doc-07-company-code-step.png|width=700!
# The status shows *On — Required for all company members*.
!doc-08-company-on.png|width=700!
# Every member is asked for the code at the next sign-in. On *Security* they see **Your company requires it** and have no switch of their own.
!doc-09-member-company-requires.png|width=700!

!doc-gif-company-requires.gif|width=700!

{panel:title=Note|bgColor=#DEEBFF}
A changed mode applies at the next password sign-in. Sessions already open, and their refresh tokens,
keep working. OTP sign-in, passkeys and API keys are not affected.
{panel}
