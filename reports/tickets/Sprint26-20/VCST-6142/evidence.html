Bug and fix

The 56 px check on a completed mission card sat on a translucent success scrim, so its contrast depended on the banner art showing through it, and fell below the WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11 minimum of 3:1. PR #2590 puts the check on an opaque success-600 disc (.mission-card__check, 64 px). The scrim and aria-hidden stay as they were.
