Bug and root cause

At 1280 px, signed in as a multi-organization user with long names, “Ship to: Select address” wrapped to two lines inside the 40 px utility bar and ran into “Call us”. The org/user names wrapper was xl:inline, so truncate and max-width on the names had no effect; the right column is shrink-0, so all the squeeze fell on the ship-to placeholder, which had no nowrap. The fix makes the wrapper xl:flex, caps the names at 128 px between 1280 and 1500 when both org and user show, and truncates the placeholder.
