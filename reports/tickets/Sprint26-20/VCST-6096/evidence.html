VCST-6096 · /qa-verify-fix · 2026-10-05

VcButton keeps its name while loading

VERIFIED · STR 3/3 · checklist 10/10

While a VcButton was loading, its label was hidden with visibility: hidden, which also removes it from the accessibility tree. The spinner has no name, so the Browse button in the Barcode scan dialog was announced as an unnamed button (axe button-name, critical). PR #2528 hides the label with opacity: 0 instead and sets aria-busy="true" while loading.
