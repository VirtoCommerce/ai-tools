--- Part 0 — VALUE CHAIN ---
L1 Manager picks an audience in one of four modes (Everyone / Specific people or companies / Match by
   conditions / Advanced query); lists and conditions combine.
L2 The choice is serialised to the stored pair (`MemberQuery` phrase, `MemberIds` list).
L3 The pair is resolved LIVE into a recipient estimate via `POST preview-recipients`, shown as a headline count
   plus arithmetic (members matched / companies expanded / people from companies / people in scope / extra logins).
L4 Save persists the pair; reopening the message must restore the MODE that produced it (`detectAudienceMode`).
L5 Send resolves the SAME pair through the SAME service into concrete recipient rows (one per security account).
L6 Each recipient sees the message in the storefront inbox (xAPI `pushMessages`).          [not under test]
L7 "Keep sending to new matches" re-resolves hourly and adds newly-matching recipients.    [not under test]

**THE CENTRAL CLAIM: the number shown at L3 equals the number delivered at L5.** That is the defect the ticket
exists to fix, and it is the only thing a count is FOR. Every scenario below is falsifiable against it.
