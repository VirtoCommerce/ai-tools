## Risk Areas
- Canceled status is visible in Today, day and All views and in day counts, but the calendar hides it on canceled-only days.
- The dateless → dated edit silently lands on the 00:00 boundary, which is TZ-sensitive (#18 not reached).

## Observations
- **Retracted:** an early save on a probe canceled row returned `errors:["Task not found."]`. The data engineer had deleted that row in the same minute (All 16→14). It does not reproduce on seeded Kilo. The kb entry KB-CE9257BC is disputed by its author.
- `?filter=zzz` normalises to Today and the URL is cleaned. `/company/calendar` shows "404 Page not found". Double-click on an action sends 1 mutation (BL-SR-030 holds). Rail grid keys (Arrow, Home, Enter) work. Chip click-back restores the day and month. × target is 24×24.
- Overdue sublines read "Expired <date>" (as in the mockup). A canceled past-due task reads "Due <date>".
- The `ru` locale localizes the calendar but the heading, date chip and sublines show "Oct 14, 2026". This is storefront-wide (customer orders too), not this PR. The week starts Mon in `ru` and Sun in en-US (the mockup shows Mon).
- No console errors and no GraphQL `errors[]` on Tasks, dashboard, my-customers or customer orders. The only console errors are catalog image 404s on /catalog (out of scope).
