## Risk Areas
- Cart identity: the storefront and createPunchoutRequisition resolve "the cart" differently. Any named cart from any channel splits them.
- Money: Total sums unrounded ExtendedPrice, while UnitPrice is rounded to 2 decimals.

## Observations
- Cart-level rewards (SUPER 20%, FIXED5 $5) are never allocated into PlacedPrice; cXML Total excludes both. The 1c "cart % allocated" was a line-level RewardItemGetOfRel (QA Category Percent).
- Decimal separator stays a dot under de-DE (152.00, 79.99).
- No SharedSecret, session id or token appears in the posted cXML; the Sender is the supplier identity.
- Config-0 allow-list refused 7 host and scheme variants (400); host case is ignored.
- At 375 px the button sits in a sticky bottom bar and is disabled in the same way as on desktop.
- Quote request ("Add cart items to quote") is still offered in punchout mode.
- The "Complete all required information" hint shows while the button is enabled.
- The rendered button carries `data-test-id="place-order-button"`, not `create-requisition-button`.
