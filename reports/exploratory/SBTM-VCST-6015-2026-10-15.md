## Risk Areas
- The buyer-facing `/company/sales-reps` is delivered by the plugin too. A rep-only feature failure also removes a page every org buyer has (3x-7).
- Every visitor, anonymous and buyer, downloads the plugin: 13 resources, ~51 KB transferred (cold, anonymous), ~208 KB decoded (H2).

## Observations
- #3 cold deep link, rep, 5 hub URLs, polled every 100 ms: no Not Found frame. Real page in 1.07–1.64 s.
- AC-3, buyer, the same 5 URLs: replaced to `/account/dashboard` in 1.02–1.21 s. No Not Found frame, no `salesRep*` operation. Back goes to the previous page; no loop.
- #19 slow remoteEntry (15 s): "Loading page…" at 2.5 s, the slow notice + Reload at ≤8.5 s, the hub at ~19 s.
- AC-2.b, en: the Corporate link reads **"Sales reps"** and the page h1 is "Sales reps" (shown uppercase). It does not say "My Sales Reps".
- U-2: exactly 1 Corporate "Sales reps" link and 1 "Sales Rep hub" section on desktop en, after an SPA switch en→de ("Vertriebsmitarbeiter", "Vertriebsmitarbeiter-Hub") and in the 375px menu.
- H5: My customers "My last order" 10/13/2026 = order details "Created: 10/13/2026". This is not a UTC-boundary order, so the off-by-one-day hypothesis is not exercised.
- de rail shows "Meine kunden" (lowercase noun). Possible locale typo, low.
- `/company/sales-reps` document.title stays "Virto Commerce". This adds a surface to the known KB-52188DBA.
- Admin settings are never fetched at runtime, so V10 cannot be overridden on the stand.

## Questions
- Is a 404 page the intended state when the manifest fails, or should it be the Reload offer (as when remoteEntry fails)? The model's D3 diagram allows both.
- Should a plugin delivery failure send a telemetry event? Today the HTML case is fully silent.
- My customers "Last order" links to `/account/orders/{id}`, the buyer route. Is this intended for a rep?
