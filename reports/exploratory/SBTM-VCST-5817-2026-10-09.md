## Risk Areas
- Every slug transition removes the FOOTER while the matcher loader shows (layout-shift 0.213, hri=false), outside the header.
- Loader length depends on the target page's fetch (3.3 s for an empty category), so the pending-submit window is not small.

## Observations
- The loader's a11y name is "Loading search context" [disabled], aria-busy=true. The invisible old label is still in the tree as button content (`button "Loading search context": Laser Monochrome`). Visually hidden in light and dark (`loader-pending-searchbar.png`, `dark-loader-pending.png`).
- A valid in-store `/category/<id>` redirects to its slug (loader 80 to chip, min slot 80).
- Back clears an unsubmitted typed query, and focus leaves the input.
- Console: only env noise (404 images, App Insights). No JS errors and no 4xx/5xx on localhost GraphQL.
