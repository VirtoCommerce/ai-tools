The bug and the fix

On mobile order cards, a long order number wrapped to two lines and squeezed the Total, which then broke inside the number ($247.0 / 0) and read as a different amount. The fix stops the Total from shrinking or wrapping (white-space: nowrap, flex-shrink: 0) and adds an 8 px gap, so the order number wraps instead. It applies to the customer Orders page and the Orders widget.
