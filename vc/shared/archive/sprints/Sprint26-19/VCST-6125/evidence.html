VCST-6125: a co-owner can no longer take over an organization list

vcptcore-qa · verified 2026-10-05 · RED baseline from the VCST-5707 run of 2026-09-30 on XCart 3.1037.0-pr-141-fb27 (pre-fix)

Platform 3.1076.0XCart 3.1038.0-pr-141-b404Cart 3.1011.0-pr-194-8331 SalesRep 3.1012.0-pr-21-8964Theme 2.59.0-pr-2476-0abbPRs x-cart #141 · vc-frontend #2476

Bug and fix

On an Organization-scope list every organization member has Write access. Before the fix, changeWishlist with any scope from a member re-assigned the owner to the caller, so the original owner lost the list. With AnyoneAnonymous the list also became readable by anyone with the key. A member could also delete the owner's list.

The fix in x-cart #141 refuses scope and sharing writes and removeWishlist from anyone but the owner. The storefront (vc-frontend #2476) now offers only Rename to a non-owner.
