Bug and fix

A refused scope change (Customer with no targets) mutated the CartAggregate held in the cache before validation threw. Later reads served the rejected state and the next rename persisted Customer / targets: [], which broke the public link. Fix: SalesRep's Customer policy now validates before writing (ff6da14), and XCart evicts the cached aggregate if any scope policy throws (cd984e3).
